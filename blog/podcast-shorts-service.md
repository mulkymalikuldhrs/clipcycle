# Why Your Podcast Isn't on Shorts (And The 48-Hour Fix)

You recorded 60 minutes. It's on Spotify. Maybe Apple. Done, right?

Wrong. You left 90% of the reachable audience on the table.

## The short-form gap

TikTok, Reels, and YouTube Shorts have different discovery mechanics than podcast apps. A listener finds you by *accident* on Shorts. They never would have searched your show name.

But cutting a 9:16 clip from a 60-minute MP3 is annoying:
- Find the moment (tedious)
- Reformat to vertical (tools)
- Add captions (more tools)
- Hook in first 2 seconds (craft)
- Export 10 versions (time)

That's 3–4 hours per episode if you do it yourself. At founder rates, that's $150–400 of your time for maybe 3 clips.

1. **Self-contained moment** — a point that lands without context.
2. **Hook in 2s** — the face or the bold text appears immediately.
3. **Captions styled bold** — 85% watch muted.
4. **One idea per clip** — not a summary, a spike.
