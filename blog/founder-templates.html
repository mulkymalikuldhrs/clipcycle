3 Templates That Killed My Reactive Days

I used to open my laptop and immediately react. Slack, email, "urgent" things. By noon I'd shipped nothing that mattered.

These three templates fixed it. Free, no email gate.

1 · Solo Founder Daily OS

One field matters: **Top Outcome**. If the day has no single most important thing by 10am, the day is reactive. Fix or accept.

Plus a shutdown ritual: 3 wins, 1 friction, set tomorrow's outcome. Five minutes. Non-negotiable.

2 · Content Pipeline Tracker

Idea → Research → Draft → Edit → Scheduled → Published.

The rule that changed everything: **max 20 backlog items**. Full backlog = stop consuming, start shipping. Every Sunday delete the bottom 30%.

3 · Trading Journal Lite

Pre-trade checklist where every box must be YES or it's NO TRADE. R-multiple logging. Rule-violation governor: >0 violations → next week size halved.

Compatible field structure if you run a systematic strategy.
